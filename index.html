How to use this register

  1. Set up once — fill in the centre details above, then add every item in Item Master with its opening stock.
  2. Every day — record each stock movement in Receipt / Issue: receipts with the bill number, issues with the department or indent number, and anything expired or damaged as a write-off.
  3. End of the month — open Monthly Stock, count the shelves, enter the physical count beside the book balance, give a reason for any difference, then press Close & Save Month. The physical count becomes next month's opening.
  4. Then print — go to Print Reports, choose the monthly register, print it on A4 landscape and paste it into the register book. Get it signed.
  5. Finally — take a backup and keep the file safe.
