How to use this register

  1. Set up once — fill in the office details above. Then enter every employee in Staff & Dependents together with the family covered by his card, and put the empanelled hospitals into Empanelled Hospitals. The whole hospital list can be uploaded from a spreadsheet instead of being typed — press Sample list to see the columns it expects.
  2. When an empanelment is extended — press Extend against the hospital and enter the new date and the order number. The period in force moves forward and the one it replaced is kept on file, so an old bill can still be checked against the empanelment that applied on the day of treatment.
  3. As claims come in — enter them in Medical Claims. A hospital claim is cashless treatment settled with the hospital; an individual reimbursement is money paid back to the employee. Fill in the amount claimed when the bill arrives and the amount reimbursed when it is passed, and the outstanding balance follows on its own.
  4. Every month — print or download the Monthly Claim Register, and the two reimbursement reports for what is payable to the hospitals and to the staff.
  5. Every year — print or download the Annual Claim Statement, which sets the year out month by month with cashless against reimbursement.
  6. When somebody asks — the Individual Staff Claim Statement answers what one employee has drawn, and the Hospital-wise Claim Statement answers what one hospital has billed.
  7. Finally — take a backup and keep the file safe.
